# 1. Ideology
Free distribution will be targeted for power users in order to encourage them to contribute improvements or insights that were not considered during testing. The overall idea is to build everything around an **Open Core** model (mainly because I dislike proprietary software and this is just for a school project).

# 2. Products
Subscription services and access to the AI API will be provided with different plans offering varied limits and features (this is primarily to maintain servers, external LLM APIs, and the database).

# 3. Pricing
Subscriptions range from 50 to 600 Mexican Pesos (the latter being essentially an unlimited service tier).
